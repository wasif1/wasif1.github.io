Who We Are

Nine Years of Engineering Without Compromise

CodeFoundri was founded on a frustration familiar to many founders and CTOs: too many software agencies that overpromise, underdeliver, and disappear after the launch invoice is paid.

We set out to build something different — a company where senior engineers do the work, where architectural decisions are made with the client's business model in mind, and where post-launch isn't the end of the relationship, it's the beginning.

Over nine years, that philosophy has earned us a 98% client retention rate and a portfolio of 35+ products that continue to run, grow, and generate revenue for the companies we built them with.
