How It Works

Engineers Who Join Standups, Know Your Codebase, and Ship Every Sprint

This is not body-leasing. Our dedicated engineers don't just take tickets from a board — they participate in your planning, contribute to architectural decisions, and care about the product they're building for you.

You get to interview and select the engineers. They join your Slack, your Jira/Linear, your GitHub. They attend standups in your timezone. They're yours — without the full-time hiring overhead.

Most of our dedicated team clients start with one or two engineers to cover a skills gap and expand as they see the quality of the work. Average engagement length is 14 months.
