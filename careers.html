Life at CodeFoundri

What It's Actually Like to Work Here

We know every company says "great culture." Here's what we mean concretely.

Remote-First, Async-Friendly

Work from wherever you do your best thinking. We have structured async standups and overlap hours — not 9am mandatory meetings.

Learning & Growth Budget

$2,000 annual budget for courses, conferences, books, and certifications. We invest in engineers who invest in themselves.

Direct Client Exposure

Senior engineers talk directly to clients — no translation layer. You understand the business context of what you're building, which makes your work better.

No Crunch Culture

We scope projects honestly. If something is taking longer than expected, we raise it early — we don't expect engineers to make up for bad planning by working nights.
