Common Questions

Honest Answers to Hard Questions

Everything you want to know about working with CodeFoundri — cost, timelines, process, and what happens when things get complex.

💰 Cost & Budget

Project costs vary based on complexity, team size, and timeline. Simple web applications typically start around $15,000–$30,000. Complex SaaS platforms range from $50,000–$200,000+. AI-integrated or enterprise products can exceed $500,000. We provide detailed, fixed-scope estimates during discovery — no surprises.
Both, depending on project type. For projects with well-defined scope, we offer fixed-price contracts. For evolving products or dedicated team engagements, we work on monthly retainers. We'll recommend the model that best protects your budget and our ability to deliver quality.
Never. Our proposals include everything — infrastructure setup, third-party API integrations (unless costs are external), QA, deployment, and documentation. If scope changes mid-project, we surface the cost impact immediately and get written approval before proceeding.

⏱ Timelines & Delivery

An MVP typically takes 8–16 weeks. A full-featured SaaS platform may take 4–9 months. Enterprise systems with complex integrations can take 9–18 months. We break every project into two-week sprints so you see real, working software regularly — not at the very end.
Yes. If you have a hard deadline (investor demo, product launch, conference), tell us upfront. We'll scope to fit it — and be honest if what you want can't be built safely in the available time. We'd rather scope an excellent MVP than rush a full product.
We surface timeline risks as early as possible — never at the deadline. If scope changes from your side cause delays, we discuss options openly: reduce scope, extend timeline, or add resources. For delays on our side, we absorb the cost.

👥 Team & Process

Senior engineers with 5+ years of production experience. You'll meet the team during onboarding and have direct access to them throughout the project — not through an account manager layer. We don't staff junior engineers on client projects.
We deliberately structure overlap hours to cover US Eastern, US Pacific, UK, EU, and Gulf time zones. Your project manager is always reachable within 4 hours during business days. We use Slack, Notion, and Loom — you'll never feel out of the loop.
Both. We've built first MVPs for pre-seed founders and scaled platforms for Series B companies. Our process adapts — early-stage projects move faster and lean on our product judgment more; mature companies get more rigorous architecture reviews and compliance work.

🤖 AI & Mobile

Yes. We integrate GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, and open-source LLMs into existing systems. Common integrations include RAG knowledge retrieval, AI chat interfaces, document processing, automated workflows, and computer vision. We start with an audit to identify the highest-ROI integration points before writing any code.
Flutter delivers more consistent UI across platforms and better performance for complex UI. React Native has a larger ecosystem and easier code sharing with web teams already using React. We'll recommend the right choice based on your UX requirements, team context, and long-term platform plans — not what's trendy.
A simple cross-platform MVP starts around $20,000–$40,000. A feature-rich consumer app with backend infrastructure typically runs $60,000–$150,000. Enterprise apps with complex integrations can be $200,000+. We always provide a detailed breakdown before starting.

🔒 Security, Ownership & Support

Absolutely. Full IP ownership transfers to you upon project completion and final payment. You get access to all repositories from day one — not just at the end. We use GitHub or your preferred version control system.
Security is built in, not bolted on. Our process includes OWASP Top 10 compliance, dependency auditing with automated CVE scanning, penetration testing before launch, encrypted data at rest and in transit, and proper secrets management. For regulated industries (healthcare, fintech), we follow HIPAA and PCI-DSS requirements.
All projects include a 30-day post-launch support window at no extra cost. After that, we offer monthly maintenance retainers, feature development packages, and dedicated team extensions. 78% of our clients continue working with us after initial project delivery.
Yes. We start with a codebase audit to assess technical debt, performance bottlenecks, and security gaps. Then we create a prioritized remediation plan. We've taken over several products built by other agencies and successfully scaled them — it's something we do regularly.
