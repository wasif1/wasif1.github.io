Our Philosophy

Why We Don't Chase Every New Framework

We choose boring technology where appropriate, exciting technology where it genuinely helps, and always prioritize long-term maintainability over short-term novelty.

Production-Proven

Every technology we recommend has been battle-tested in real production environments — not just tutorials and side projects.

Hire-able Ecosystem

We choose technologies with large, active communities so you can hire engineers to maintain and extend your codebase after launch.

Right Tool for the Job

We don't have a hammer looking for nails. We match the technology to the problem — sometimes that means boring SQL, sometimes it means cutting-edge AI.

Future-Proof Architecture

We abstract infrastructure concerns properly so replacing a component later doesn't require rewriting your entire application.

Have a Preferred Stack?

We adapt to your existing technology choices. If you've already committed to a stack, we'll work within it — and tell you honestly if we see any issues worth addressing.
