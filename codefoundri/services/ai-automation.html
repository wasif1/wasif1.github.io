AI Capabilities

What We Actually Build With AI

AI is only valuable when it works reliably in production. Here are the categories of AI work we do regularly for clients.

LLM Integration & Fine-Tuning

GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, and open-source model integration. Prompt engineering, context management, and output validation for reliable production behavior.

RAG Knowledge Systems

Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipelines that ground LLM responses in your specific documents, policies, product data, or knowledge base — with verifiable citations.

AI Agents & Workflows

Multi-step AI agents that research, decide, act, and retry — integrated with your existing tools via APIs. Automate complex workflows that previously required human judgment.

Document Processing

Intelligent extraction from invoices, contracts, forms, and PDFs. Structured data output, validation, and integration with downstream systems.

AI Customer Support

Omnichannel AI support systems with escalation logic, CRM integration, and CSAT measurement. Handles Tier 1 support with human handoff for complex cases.

Business Process Automation

End-to-end workflow automation connecting your existing tools — CRM, ERP, email, Slack, payment systems — with AI decision-making at the critical junctions.

Computer Vision

Image classification, object detection, OCR, and visual quality inspection systems for manufacturing, healthcare imaging, and document processing use cases.

Predictive Analytics

ML models for churn prediction, demand forecasting, anomaly detection, and customer segmentation — integrated directly into your product or dashboard.

Our Approach

AI That Works in Production, Not Just Demos

Audit Before Building

We identify your highest-ROI AI integration points before writing code. Many companies waste months building impressive demos that don't impact the bottom line.

Evaluation Frameworks

Every AI feature ships with an evaluation suite — not just "does it seem to work" but measurable accuracy, latency, and cost metrics you can monitor over time.

Graceful Degradation

AI systems fail in interesting ways. We build fallbacks, confidence thresholds, and human-in-the-loop escalation paths so your product works even when the AI doesn't.

Cost Management

LLM API costs can spiral without proper token management, caching, and model selection strategy. We build cost-efficient systems that don't surprise you at the end of the month.
