Our Process

A Process Designed for
Predictable Outcomes

No black boxes. No "trust us, it's almost done." Here's exactly how we take a product from idea to production — and what you get at each step.

01

Discovery & Scoping

Duration: 1–2 weeks

This is where most projects succeed or fail — and where most agencies cut corners. We invest heavily in discovery because a poorly scoped project is a guaranteed overrun.

We map your business model to your technical requirements, identify the riskiest assumptions in your product, surface the integration complexity early, and define success metrics before starting. The output is a document you could hand to any engineering team and get the same result.

What you receive

  • Product requirements document
  • Technical scope specification
  • Risk & dependency register
  • Project timeline with milestones
  • Fixed-price proposal
  • Team introduction & onboarding plan
02

Architecture & Design

Duration: 1–3 weeks

Before writing production code, we design the system. This means choosing the right database schema, defining the API contract, planning the service boundaries, and designing the UX flows that your team approves.

Architecture decisions made here are 10x cheaper to change than after implementation. We treat this phase with the seriousness it deserves — including documenting the decisions we considered and why we chose what we chose.

What you receive

  • System architecture diagram
  • Database schema (ERD)
  • API contract (OpenAPI spec)
  • UX wireframes / flows
  • Infrastructure plan
  • Security model
03

Agile Development

Duration: Ongoing (2-week sprints)

Development runs in two-week sprints. At the end of each sprint, you get a deployed staging environment with working features — not just a status report. You test it, give feedback, and we iterate in the next sprint.

You have direct access to the engineering team via Slack. Daily async standups are shared in writing. Nothing is hidden until it's "perfect" — you see the product evolving in real time.

What you receive each sprint

  • Working software on staging
  • Sprint demo recording (Loom)
  • Updated progress tracker (Notion)
  • Next sprint preview & priorities
  • Any scope changes surfaced early
  • Code committed to your repository
04

QA & Security Review

Duration: 1–3 weeks before launch

Before anything reaches production, it goes through a structured QA process. This isn't just clicking through the UI — it includes automated test suites, load testing, and a security review.

We don't ship until we're confident in it. Our QA process is the reason our clients rarely deal with critical production incidents after launch.

What's covered

  • Functional testing (manual + automated)
  • Cross-browser & device testing
  • Load & stress testing
  • OWASP security audit
  • Dependency vulnerability scan
  • Performance benchmarking
05

Production Launch

Duration: 1–2 weeks

Launch is a structured process, not a moment. We set up CI/CD pipelines for zero-downtime deployments, configure monitoring and alerting, document the deployment runbook, and execute the production rollout systematically.

We're available throughout launch week — and for 30 days after — to handle any production issues that arise.

What's included

  • CI/CD pipeline setup
  • Infrastructure provisioning
  • Monitoring & alerting (Prometheus/Grafana)
  • Deployment runbook
  • 30-day post-launch support
  • Codebase & API documentation
06

Growth & Iteration

Duration: Ongoing

78% of our clients continue working with us after launch — because shipping v1 is just the beginning. We offer roadmap planning, feature development sprints, and dedicated team arrangements for companies that want to keep moving fast.

We also proactively monitor your production systems and surface performance or security issues before they become incidents.

Post-launch options

  • Feature sprint packages
  • Monthly maintenance retainer
  • Dedicated engineering team
  • Technical CTO advisory
  • Roadmap planning sessions
  • Performance optimization
